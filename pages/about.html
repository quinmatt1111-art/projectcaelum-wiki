Project Caelum is a combat space simulator set in an alternate timeline where the space race accelerated instead of winding down.

The archive

This wiki is presented as the internal records system of the United States Aerospace Command (AEROCOM), established in the 1970s. Records were created over several decades and many were never migrated, so older entries still look the way they did on the system they were written on:

  • ADN Mainframe (1970s) – green-screen terminal records
  • AEROCOM Web (1990s) – the first internal web server
  • AEROCOM Portal (2000s) – the intranet portal era
  • Current System – the present-day archive

Classification markings

Every record carries a banner at the top and bottom showing its overall classification. Paragraphs may carry their own markings, and some details are redacted.
